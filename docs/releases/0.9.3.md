A patch release for the streaming host: a TV that joins while the host's screen is still gets its picture at once, not a green screen for up to 20 seconds.

The fix needs **StrmLight 0.9.4** on the TV. A StrmLight 0.9.2 or 0.9.3 still streams from StrmQt 0.9.3 and plays together as before; it just keeps the old behaviour on join.

## After updating

If you use the streaming host, open Settings → StrmLight → Streaming host and press **Restart** once, then update each TV to StrmLight 0.9.4.

## What's new

- **A TV that joins gets its picture.** The desktop sends the streaming host a new frame only when something on the screen changes, so a TV that joined while the screen was still could stay green until something moved, for up to 20 seconds, most often soon after the host started. The host already drew its screen once when a TV joined, but that could come before the TV's stream was ready for it. StrmLight 0.9.4 now tells the host, four times a second, while it has no picture yet, and the host draws its screen again each time until it has.

## Known limitations

Everything listed for [0.9.2](https://github.com/mpengellyCA/strmQt-linux/blob/v0.9.3/docs/releases/0.9.2.md#known-limitations) still applies. Also:

- **A short stall every few minutes on a slower host.** On a mini PC with integrated graphics streaming to four TVs, every TV's picture has been seen to stop for about half a second every three or four minutes. It is not caused by joining and not by the network; it is being looked into.
