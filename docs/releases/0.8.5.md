YouTube and YouTube Music join the menu: two new destinations, driven with the same remote as the rest of StrmQt, that keep playing while you browse.

## What's new

- **YouTube.** YouTube's own TV interface is a destination in the menu, driven with the same arrows, OK and Back as everything else. A video takes the whole window; press Menu, or touch the left edge with the pointer, and the app's menu slides out over it.
- **YouTube Music.** The full music site, drawn large, with a cursor the arrows move to the nearest song, album or button.
- **Background play.** Leave either page and what it was playing carries on under a docked bar like the one a film gets: scrub, volume, stop, and previous and next for music. A YouTube video keeps its picture in a frame above the bar. Only one thing is ever heard: starting a film, a video or a track quiets the others.
- **Trailers play in the app** instead of opening a browser.
- **Full screen** can be toggled from the profile menu.
- **Off switches.** Settings → Server → YouTube turns each of the two off separately. Off removes it from the menu, stops it, and sends trailers back to your browser.

## Signing in to YouTube

- **YouTube** signs in on its own screen with a code, as on a TV.
- **YouTube Music** cannot be signed in to inside an app, because Google refuses it. Instead, sign in to YouTube in Firefox, then open Settings → Server → YouTube Music sign-in, press **Look in Firefox**, and choose the Firefox profile or container to use.
- StrmQt opens nothing of Firefox's until you press that button. It then copies the `youtube.com` and `google.com` cookies of the profile you pick, which is the Google session of every account signed in there, into its own web profile. It never writes to Firefox's files.
- Only Firefox is supported. Chrome-family browsers keep their cookies encrypted.

There is no ad blocking, and none is planned.

## New dependencies

Qt WebEngine (Chromium) and SQLite. The packages are larger for it; the Flatpak carries the engine itself. The AppImage is built without the two for now. Building from source without them: `-DSTRMQT_WITH_YOUTUBE=OFF`.

## On Ubuntu 24.04 and Debian 12

Every page and feature works on Qt 6.4. A few things are drawn slightly differently: icons, shadows and round masks use Qt's older effects module, the blurred backdrop behind Home and person pages has a different character, and the Crate music headings are narrower because Qt 6.4 cannot widen the display font.

## On Fedora

- The `.rpm` is tied to Fedora's exact Qt version. When Fedora updates Qt within a release, `dnf upgrade` holds that update back, or asks to remove StrmQt, until a StrmQt `.rpm` rebuilt for the new Qt ships. The Flatpak does not have this problem.
- HEVC and AAC need RPM Fusion's `ffmpeg` in place of Fedora's `ffmpeg-free`: `sudo dnf swap ffmpeg-free ffmpeg --allowerasing`.

## Known limitations

See [ARCHITECTURE.md §10](https://github.com/mpengellyCA/strmQt-linux/blob/v0.8.5/ARCHITECTURE.md#10-known-limitations). New in this release:

- **YouTube and YouTube Music are other people's pages.** StrmQt drives them with scripts that depend on how the two sites are built today; either can change without notice and break navigation, the docked bar's previous/next, or the left-edge handoff to the menu.
- **One day old.** These features have had one evening's use on one machine (Qt 6.12, Plasma, Wayland). On Ubuntu 24.04 and Debian 12 (Qt 6.4) they build and pass the tests but have not been used by hand.
- **YouTube Music's search box needs a keyboard.** There is no on-screen keyboard for it.
- **The copied YouTube Music sign-in expires** when Google says so; copy it again when it does.
- **The YouTube sign-ins belong to your desktop account**, not to the Emby profile: a second Emby user on the same desktop account finds them still signed in.
- **The AppImage has no YouTube or YouTube Music yet.** Every other package does.
- **Upscaling does not apply to YouTube.** The upscaling shaders are mpv's.

Carried over:

- **GNOME idle inhibition is untested.** Whether GNOME keeps the screen awake during playback was not checked for this release.
- **Offline source builds on Ubuntu 24.04 and Debian 12.** Building the `.deb` from source downloads one pinned, hash-checked SDL3 tarball, so an offline build will not work there. Installing the binary `.deb` is unaffected.
- The AppImage does not run on Debian 12 (glibc 2.36). Use the `.deb` or the Flatpak there.
