TVs watching the streaming host together now play in step: two rooms sound as one, with no echo, and each TV keeps its picture with its sound.

Playing together needs **StrmLight 0.9.2** on every TV. A StrmLight 0.9.1 still streams from StrmQt 0.9.2, as before, just not in step with the others.

## After updating

If you use the streaming host, open Settings → StrmLight → Streaming host and press **Restart** once, then update each TV to StrmLight 0.9.2.

## What's new

### TVs play together

- **The host keeps time.** The streaming host's StrmQt answers time requests on UDP 8339, inside StrmQt's own port block, so every StrmLight knows the host's clock to within a fraction of a millisecond.
- **Every TV plays the same moment at the same time.** Each StrmLight tells the host, about once a second, how long its own path is, from the host to its speaker. The host tells every TV the same delay, the slowest TV's path plus a little margin, and each TV plays every sound and shows every picture that long after the host made it. Two TVs side by side are heard as one.
- **Picture and sound stay together.** The picture is held by the same delay as the sound, so lip sync on each TV is as it was.
- **Joining is quiet.** A TV that starts streaming while others play shows *Syncing with other devices…*, muted, for a few seconds, and comes in in step. The TVs already playing hear nothing. The host remembers each TV's path for half an hour, so a TV that comes back is already counted for.
- **The menus are not held back.** The delay applies only while the host plays something. In the menus every TV answers the remote as quickly as before.

## Known limitations

See [ARCHITECTURE.md §10](https://github.com/mpengellyCA/strmQt-linux/blob/v0.9.2/ARCHITECTURE.md#10-known-limitations). For playing together:

- **Every TV waits for the slowest.** Bluetooth speakers or a soundbar on one TV can add a few hundred milliseconds, and then every TV plays that far behind the host. Pausing and seeking feel that much slower while a video plays.
- **Starting and stopping a video is heard once on every TV**, as each moves between the menus' no-delay and the shared delay.
- **A TV slower than any the host has seen recently** raises the delay when it joins, and the others skip once.
- **It has been tried on two Fire TVs**, a Fire TV set and a Fire TV Stick, on one network.

Everything else listed for 0.9.1 still applies.
