Library: how do you move around and filter?

All three show the Albums view of the library, in the Crate style, filtered to Jazz from the 1970s. They differ in two things: where the section switcher lives (Home / Albums / Artists / Songs / Genres / Playlists), and what filtering looks like. Each replaces today's shared FilterBar. Pick one, or tell me which parts to mix.

Everything is visible at once, and A–Z works like the tab dividers in a record bin.
