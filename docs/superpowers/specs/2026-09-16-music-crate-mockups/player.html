Full-screen player: which one?

All three are the full-screen Now Playing view in the Crate style. The docked bar stays as in the Home mockup: a square cover flush to the left, links for title, artist and album, the transport controls, and a 2 px playhead along the top edge. Today's "continuous sleeve" animation still carries the cover from the bar into this view. Queue, lyrics and album are reachable in all three; they differ in what takes centre stage.

Good: the clearest hierarchy, and the closest match to Home's hero, so going from Home to the player feels like one continuous move. The tabs keep lyrics and credits a single press away without cluttering the view.

Cost: it's the safest of the three. The cover is static, so the only motion is the wash and the sleeve animation.

Good: the most memorable. The spinning disc is the "is it playing?" signal: it slows to a stop on pause and slides back into the sleeve on stop. It's pure tactile Crate.

Cost: the animation runs for as long as music plays, so it needs care on low-power hardware (one rotating layer, paused when the window is hidden). It also doesn't fit a digital-only single as well as it fits an album.
